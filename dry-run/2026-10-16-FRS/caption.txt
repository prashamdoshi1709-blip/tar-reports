The Announcement Room | Forrestania Resources (FRS)

Forrestania Resources says the Edna May gold plant could process its first ore in mid-December 2026, subject to the timely receipt and installation of final equipment components. Mill liners are due on site in the final week of November, and the first tailings storage cell is progressing toward completion in late November.

Lake Johnston, the company's second plant, is now described as starting commissioning and production in early 2027. Earlier company documents pointed to late 2026. The company has not said whether this is a delay.

Key points:
• Edna May first ore is targeted for mid-December 2026
• First ore is a different milestone from the 1H 2027 mill restart flagged in June
• Mill liners due on site in the final week of November
• Lake Johnston elution circuit hot commissioning is underway
• The release gives no tonnes, grade, cost or cash figures for the first ore
• The 18 Sep study shows A$728.7m pre-tax free cash flow at A$5,500/oz, but assumes a January 2030 start

Looking ahead, investors will watch whether the liners arrive and are fitted on time, whether ore is processed in December, which deposit supplies it, and when Lake Johnston starts.
